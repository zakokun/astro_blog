## What I work on

I like designing backend systems: defining service boundaries, making data flows reliable, and finding practical trade-offs between performance, complexity, and cost. My main tools are Go, MySQL, Elasticsearch/OpenSearch, Redis, AWS, and the usual observability and delivery stack around them.

I am particularly interested in distributed systems, search and data platforms, performance optimization, and production reliability. I also use AI coding tools for design exploration, code review, and test development, while keeping engineering judgment and verification in the loop.
