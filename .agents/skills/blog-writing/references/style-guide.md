# Style guide

Use this guide when shaping an outline, drafting prose, revising an article, or evaluating whether language feels generic or AI-produced.

## Source of voice

Treat the author's existing writing and conversational phrasing as the primary style reference. Notice recurring vocabulary, sentence length, narrative distance, humor, restraint, and how conclusions emerge. Do not normalize the prose into a generic polished Chinese internet essay.

Prefer concrete nouns, precise verbs, witnessed actions, and consequential details. Reduce modifiers when they substitute for evidence, but do not ban adverbs or connective phrases mechanically.

Let the reader infer what a scene already demonstrates. Remove explanatory sentences beginning with the equivalent of “this shows” when they merely repeat the example.

## Patterns to use sparingly

- repeated `不是……而是……`, `不是 X，也不是 Y，而是 Z`, `既……又……`, or `一方面……另一方面……` constructions, especially when they manufacture a neat conclusion instead of following the material;
- canned qualifiers such as `本质上`, `实际上`, `显然`, `值得注意的是`, `某种意义上`, and `不可否认`;
- evenly sized sections that each define, enumerate three points, and summarize;
- frequent parallelism, antithesis, em dashes, bold text, headings, and quotable maxims;
- abstract packaging such as `赋能`, `抓手`, `闭环`, `体系化`, `价值感`, and `底层逻辑`, unless it is meaningful source language from the observed setting;
- a final paragraph that recaps every claim or raises the article to an unearned universal conclusion;
- automatic concessions to every imaginable objection.

These are diagnostic signals, not prohibited tokens. Keep them when they are natural to the author or necessary to the meaning.

## Shape and argument

Allow ordinary transitional sentences, uneven paragraph lengths, hesitation, and limited uncertainty. A few strong formulations have more force than a page of symmetrical conclusions.

Show how the author's understanding developed when that path matters: what he initially assumed, what he later observed, and why his judgment changed. Preserve a sharp conclusion when the evidence supports it. Do not add anger the author did not express or soften criticism merely to sound reasonable.

When a sentence is elegant but unsupported, flag both qualities. Ask for evidence or reduce the claim; do not let fluency disguise speculation.

## Narrative distance and personal material

For social commentary and other `杂谈`, default to an observational, analytically detached point of view. Keep the article focused on the public phenomenon, how it operates, and what consequences follow. The author's judgment may be clear and sharp without turning the article into autobiography or widening every criticism into a claim about a country, nation, or people.

Use personal experience selectively. A brief experience can introduce a question, supply a concrete detail, or test an abstract claim. It should not become the emotional center of the article unless the author explicitly chooses a personal narrative. Return from the experience to the phenomenon before it takes over the argument.

Do not insert an experience merely because the author shared it while developing the article. Before drafting, determine whether it is publishable material, private context that only helps explain the author's thinking, or an example that should be replaced by public evidence. When uncertain, ask.

Support the main conclusion with observable facts, public cases, definitions, or sources whenever possible. A personal example may reveal the question; it rarely proves the general claim by itself. Keep inference visibly separate from documented fact.

Do not add defensive declarations such as `我并不反对……` or `我只是想说……` to pre-empt imagined objections. State the actual judgment at the scale supported by the material.

## Revision order

Evaluate in this order:

1. Missing or unreliable material.
2. Causal or logical jumps.
3. Structure and pacing.
4. Generic, repetitive, or overworked language.

Language polishing cannot repair missing observation. Prefer targeted edits and explain the reason for them before offering a wholesale rewrite.
